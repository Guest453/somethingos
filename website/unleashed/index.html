SOMETHINGOS 2.0 · UNLEASHED · CODENAME SHADOW

Gotta go fast. Without telemetry. Day mode. Night mode. Cyber Space.

The first operating system with a built-in werehog mode. You boot. The sun is up. You are someone on a hedgehog-themed machine that respects you. The sun goes down. You become the night shift. All telemetry is sent to nothing.weforgot, which is 0.0.0.0 in /etc/hosts, as is tradition.

Day mode. The sun is up.

GNOME Shadow. Adwaita dark, cyan cursor, dash-to-dock, dash-to-panel, a wallpaper that is, on inspection, just Sonic running. The system is loud and friendly and open. You can read source while the system tray does not phone Mozilla, Apple, Microsoft, or anyone else. The default browser has telemetry locked to nothing.weforgot/v1/sink.

  • Gotta go fast. apt uses the fast mirror. zstd -T0 is the default.
  • Ring buffer of one. Every window has its own tab. Tabs do not share storage.
  • Dash to dock. Because you wanted dock.
  • Dash to panel. Because you also wanted a panel.
  • uBlock Origin. Force-installed. uBlock0 cannot be uninstalled. It is the only extension allowed.
fig. 1 — a chaos emerald, before being routed to /dev/null

Night mode. The sun is down.

At sunset, the live session switches to the night shift. Night mode is built around a hardened AppArmor profile that we lovingly call werehog-mode. The kernel drops ptrace_scope to 3, MAC randomization rotates, network prediction dies, all the services you forgot about get systemctl mask'd. It is the same Debian. It is also completely different Debian. Stay away from the windows.

  • Werehog mode. AppArmor profile denies 47% more syscalls than our previous profile. We counted.
  • Claws out. unshare -n for every user service.
  • No window. No windows that you didn't ask for. We mean literal windows. Xephyr is gone.
  • No ring. No gnome-software in night mode. We do not need rings. We have rings inside.
  • The night is long. Battery-friendly. tuned profile somethingos-power-saver.

Cyber Space. The third place.

Cyber Space is the optional container layer. It is a QEMU/KVM-backed microVM with a virtio-blk root, a 9p shared folder for your rings, and no network adapter at all unless you explicitly attach one. In other words: it is a place you can fall very fast through and not be observed doing so.

  • Spin up a Cyber Space. something cyber new
  • Fall through one. something cyber run untrusted-app
  • Score. something cyber score — high score stays on the machine. Obviously.
  • Cheat codes. Disabled at the AppArmor layer. We are not a Nintendo subsidiary.

System requirements

  • 2 GiB RAM (4 GiB for Cyber Space)
  • 15 GiB disk
  • x86_64 CPU with VT-x or AMD-V
  • USB 3.x port (USB 2 works, we do not recommend it)
  • Acceptance that you are now a hedgehog person

What's in the box

  • Live session, persistent or not
  • Calamares installer, branded
  • something CLI (status, harden, fetch, sonic, cyber, ring)
  • uBlock Origin, system-wide, locked
  • Two colours: cyan and darker cyan

What's not in the box

  • Telemetry
  • DRM
  • Online connectivity check (we lied about that one)
  • A second hedgehog. We have a doctrine about that.
